# Mouse2Vec

The files in this directory — `pretrained_model.pkl`, `CRT.py`, `base_models.py` —
are the Mouse2Vec model and are **not** part of this project. They are included
here for convenience so the feature-extraction step runs out of the box.

Source: https://git.cai.simtech.uni-stuttgart.de/public-projects/Mouse2Vec

Please refer to the original repository for the authoritative version, license,
and citation. The copy bundled here may lag behind upstream.
